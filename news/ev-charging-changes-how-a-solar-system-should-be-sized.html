EV Charging Changes How a Solar System Should Be Sized

EV Charging Changes How a Solar System Should Be Sized

Posted by Acme Solar on 2026-02-11

An electric vehicle can represent a substantial addition to household electricity use, and charging patterns determine whether that load lands during solar generating hours or overnight. Sizing an array against historical bills alone will understate requirements if an EV is added afterwards. Where a vehicle purchase is anticipated, it is worth modelling the expected charging load at design stage rather than revisiting the system a year later.
