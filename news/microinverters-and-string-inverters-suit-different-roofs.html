Microinverters and String Inverters Suit Different Roofs

Microinverters and String Inverters Suit Different Roofs

Posted by Acme Solar on 2026-05-08

String inverters handle a group of panels together, which is efficient and cost-effective on an unshaded roof with a consistent orientation. Where shading is uneven or panels face different directions, module-level electronics allow each panel to operate independently so one shaded unit does not drag down its neighbours. Neither is universally better; the shading survey carried out during assessment is what should decide it.
